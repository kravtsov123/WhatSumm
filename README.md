## English

WhatSumm is a free Android app that summarizes your WhatsApp chats: a daily or weekly summary for each chat you choose, what needs your attention, upcoming events, and answers to questions like "When is the meeting?". Summaries are written by Google Gemini with your own free key.

### What you need

- An Android 10+ phone with WhatsApp.
- A Google account (for a free Gemini key, 2 minutes).

### Install

1. On the phone, open the [download page](https://github.com/kravtsov123/WhatSumm/releases/latest) and tap the file ending in **.apk** (under Assets).
2. Open the downloaded file. If it says your phone isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this source: tap **Settings** → turn on **Allow from this source** → go back.
3. Tap **Install**. If Google Play Protect warns that the app is unknown: tap **More details** → **Install anyway**. The warning appears because the app doesn't come from the Play Store.

### First setup

1. **Notification access:** open WhatSumm → on the first card tap **Open settings** → pick WhatSumm and turn it on.
   If the switch is grey or says "Restricted setting": **phone Settings → Apps → WhatSumm → ⋮ (top corner) → Allow restricted settings**, confirm with your PIN, then turn it on.
2. **Battery:** if the battery card shows, tap **Open app settings** → **Battery** → **Unrestricted**, or Android may stop the app.
3. **Free Gemini key:** in the **Settings** tab tap **Get a free key** → sign in with Google → **Create API key** → copy → paste it in Settings → **Save**.
4. **Your name:** in Settings, type your name as it appears in WhatsApp, so the app knows which messages are meant for you.
5. **Choose chats:** chats appear in the **Chats** tab as messages arrive. Open one and choose **Daily** or **Weekly**. Allow notifications when asked.

### What the app can't see

The app reads WhatsApp's notifications, so it doesn't see:
- muted chats (WhatsApp shows no notification for them),
- messages you send,
- messages that arrive while that chat is open on screen.

**To catch up:** in WhatsApp open the chat → ⋮ → **More** → **Export chat** → choose **WhatSumm catch-up**. You get a summary of what's new since last time.

### Privacy

- Messages are stored only on your phone and deleted automatically after 30 days. There is no server and no account.
- To make a summary, that chat's messages are sent to Google Gemini with your key. On the free tier, Google may use the content to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Don't turn on summaries for sensitive chats.
- **Settings → Delete all data** erases everything from the phone.

### Updates

When a new version comes out, the app shows a notice. Tap **Download**, open the file and tap **Update**. All your data is kept.
